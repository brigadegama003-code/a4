Refund Policy

Editorial site and future transactions

Purpose and scope

At present, Stylish Jacket Avenue is an editorial reference site and does not directly sell jackets, subscriptions or paid downloads. Because no checkout is offered, there is currently no payment to refund. Product cards are educational references rather than offers for sale, and article links remain within this publication unless clearly marked otherwise.

What you should know

If a future paid service is introduced, the applicable price, currency, deliverables, cancellation window and refund conditions will be shown clearly before payment. Those transaction-specific terms will control. Purchases completed on an external retailer are governed by that retailer’s own return and refund policy; contact the seller listed on your receipt rather than this editorial site.

Your choices and responsibilities

Never send card details or banking credentials by email or through the demonstration contact form. If you believe someone is falsely claiming to collect payment on our behalf, stop the transaction and contact us through the published address. We will update this page before enabling direct transactions. Nothing here limits mandatory consumer rights that apply under relevant law.

Recognising a genuine transaction

A genuine future transaction would show a complete order summary, seller identity, total cost and applicable policy before confirmation. An article title, collection card or contact-form button is not a checkout. We do not request payment by unsolicited message, gift card, cryptocurrency transfer or remote-access software. Readers should independently verify any external merchant and use a payment method that offers appropriate records and protections.

If money was sent to an unrelated party, contact the payment provider promptly and preserve receipts, messages and page addresses. We cannot reverse or investigate a transaction that we did not process, but reports of impersonation can help us publish a warning or take reasonable protective steps. Any future approved refund would normally return through the original payment route unless law or the provider requires another method.

Questions and updates

For questions about this policy, contact hello@stylishjacketavenue.com. We may revise this page when our services, legal requirements or operating practices change. Material updates will be dated here so readers can identify the version currently in effect.
